## Recommended PowerPoint sequence

1. Title and problem-statement alignment.
2. Idea-validation verdict: promising concept, with stakeholder/data/model validation still required.
3. System flowchart: Import → Validate → Normalise → Scenario → Optimise → Verify → Human Review → Replan → Audit/Export.
4. Feature inventory: distinguish source-present modules from seeded/static values and unverified connectors.
5. Algorithmic approach: deterministic heuristic and independent rule-based validation.
6. Public data strategy: provenance, timestamps, freshness and limitations.
7. ML/DL audit: current evidence does not establish an implemented/trained model.
8. Prediction pipeline roadmap: exact dataset, preprocessing, leakage-safe split, trained baseline/candidate, held-out metrics.
9. Feasibility and engineering acceptance gates.
10. Impact/benefits and how they will be measured.
11. SWOT and risk mitigation.
12. Business/revenue hypotheses and market-validation plan.
13. Stakeholder consultation plan and future field-validation status.
14. Prototype walkthrough video.

## Claim discipline

- The source ZIP and its exported screenshots were reviewed. The hosted AI Studio URL did not render through the available retrieval path, so the video is a **narrated screenshot-based walkthrough**, not a live browser recording.
- The supplied source contains a deterministic scheduling heuristic and rule-based validation. No reproducible ML/DL training or inference pipeline was found in the reviewed source paths.
- The prediction UI's RMSE/MAE, interval and percentage-improvement values appear as fixed data in the supplied source. Do not describe them as independently reproduced test results.
- The field-visit report is explicitly a **pre-visit / desk-research plan and fill-in template**. No office visit, interview, employee statement or endorsement is claimed because no genuine visit evidence was supplied. Fill visit dates, attendees, notes and approved photographs only after an actual authorised visit.
- Keep any public demo non-operational, human-reviewed and based on generic synthetic or authorised public data. Do not claim Ministry of Defence approval or live military-system integration.
