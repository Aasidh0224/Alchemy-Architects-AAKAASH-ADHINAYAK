# Revenue Model Report

## Status
Revenue options are hypotheses for a future general-purpose civilian/educational planning product. The current repository has no paying customers, validated demand, paid integration or confirmed unit economics.

## Candidate models
1. **Education / research licence:** curriculum, instructor materials, sandbox datasets and assessment support.
2. **Hosted subscription:** only after repeat usage and a measurable user problem are established.
3. **Private deployment / integration services:** possible for approved civilian workflows once security, support and maintenance scope are costed.
4. **Research partnerships or grants:** support for validated experiments, data governance and responsible innovation.
5. **Training / workshops:** sessions on data provenance, constraint modelling, validation and reproducible evaluation.

## Cost categories
- Hosting, database, backups and monitoring.
- External model/API usage if integrated in a future version.
- Data licensing or access, retention and processing.
- Engineering, test infrastructure, security review and accessibility.
- Customer support, deployment maintenance and incident handling.

## Unit-economics formulas
- Monthly recurring revenue = paying accounts × account price.
- Gross margin = (revenue − direct delivery cost) ÷ revenue.
- Monthly contribution = price − attributable hosting/API/support expense.
- Payback period = acquisition cost ÷ monthly contribution.

These are modelling formulas, not a forecast. Prices should only be tested after user research, cost measurement, data/IP review and a clearly scoped pilot.

## Responsible positioning
Do not market the current demo as an approved defence product. Keep any future commercial use within a clearly defined, authorised and non-operational scope unless separate legal and domain approvals are obtained.
